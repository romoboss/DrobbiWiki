**romoboss** is the primary developer of [Drobbi Bird](?page=about) and owner of [romoboss.com](https://romoboss.com)

## Stages of Development

romoboss began his game development journey in 2020 on the platform [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) under the username romoboy29. 

The following year, romoboss created an [itch.io profile under the same username](https://romoboy29.itch.io). On this page he would upload his creations developed using the program [Construct](https://construct.net). Most popular games featured on the profile included "the realy hard game", "cube falling" and "slime jump", though some of these games may not be publically accessible anymore.

Though after some time, romoboss got bored of visual programming and wanted to try developing in an actual programming language, so he created the [Roblox](https://roblox.com) account [@romoboy29](https://www.roblox.com/users/1723740354/profile) (later renamed to @romobosss) and in 2022, after learning the basics of the luau programming language, he started working on his first Roblox experiences. As of 2026, romoboss is still active on this Roblox account and continues to work on new projects under his community [romoboss's hub](https://www.roblox.com/communities/9584350).

After learning how to work in Roblox Studio, romoboss wanted to start creating proper games, so in 2023, he created and published his first and currently only maintained Unity project - [Drobbi Bird](?page=about). Drobbi Bird was initially published on itch.io under a brand new profile named ["romoboss29"](https://romoboss.itch.io) (later only "romoboss").

Alongside developing Drobbi Bird, romoboss also works on many side projects such as his website [romoboss.com](https://romoboss.com), his Discord Bot Application ["DrobbiBot"](https://github.com/romoboss/drobbibot) or his Minecraft Fabric Mod titled ["The Flatworld"](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-flatworld).
