Gamemodes are different ways of playing [Drobbi Bird](?page=about). All of them can be played infinitely and your goal in them is to survive the longest and get the highest score. Gamemodes can be accessed through the main menu by clicking on the gamemodes button (purple button with a console controller drawn on it) and selecting the desired gamemode in the gamemode picker.

#### The game as of update 1.611 features 13 gamemodes:

- Normal
- Reversed
- Speed Up
- Easy
- Hard
- Jetpack
- Cloud Avoid
- Coin Grabber
- Random
- Just Pipes
- Trivia
- Night
- Sea
